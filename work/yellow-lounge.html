Yellow Lounge

Yellow Lounge was born in the Berlin club scene to introduce a new idea of classical music accessible by everyone. During these unique live concerts, classical music meets VJ performances in urban spaces and night clubs.

For the 2nd edition of Yellow Lounge Paris @ Batofar in 2013, the visual agency Pixel Carré has chosen the minimalism to accompany the virtuosity of violinist Nemanja Radulovic. My role was to develop a custom software that reacts to the live music and addresses the light scenography in order to generate real-time light animations in space.
