PANORAMA

PANORAMA was an independent art programme hosted by Genesis Cinema, the home of independent film in East London, to offer a platform for emerging artists to exhibit and discuss their work, develop new networks and get inspired.

The programme featured artworks at the intersection of film screenings, audiovisual performances and art exhibitions. Selected artists were also invited to participate in discussion panels with the audience to share insights about their practice and activate inspiring conversations.

The 4th edition of PANORAMA was featured as part of the East End Film Festival 2016.
